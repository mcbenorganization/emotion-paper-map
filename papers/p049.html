????? ?To address these challenges, we propose EmoSync, a multi-stage reasoning framework for more reliable and accuracy emotion recognition. (1) Alongside standard multimodal inference, we perform auxiliary unimodal reasoning, analyzing each modality (e.g., text, audio, image) separately to capture diverse emotional cues. (2) We propose a logical self-correction mechanism that leverages chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting to ensure self-consistent outputs, thereby preventing the co-occurrence of emotionally opposite terms in a single prediction. (3) We design an ensemble-of-perspectives strategy, where a large language model (LLM) aggregates predictions from multiple models based on their corresponding evaluation scores, enhancing reliability and reducing emotional conflicts.?
