摘要证据： “To address these challenges, we propose EmoSync, a multi-stage reasoning framework for more reliable and accuracy emotion recognition. (1) Alongside standard multimodal inference, we perform auxiliary unimodal reasoning, analyzing each modality (e.g., text, audio, image) separately to capture diverse emotional cues. (2) We propose a logical self-correction mechanism that leverages chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting to ensure self-consistent outputs, thereby preventing the co-occurrence of emotionally opposite terms in a single prediction. (3) We design an ensemble-of-perspectives strategy, where a large language model (LLM) aggregates predictions from multiple models based on their corresponding evaluation scores, enhancing reliability and reducing emotional conflicts.”
为了应对这些挑战,我们提出EmoSync,一个多阶段推理框架,用于更可靠和更准确的情感识别. (1)除了标准的多模式推论外,我们进行辅助的单模式推理,分别分析每一种模式(如文本,音频,图像)来捕捉不同的情感提示. (2)我们提出逻辑自我修正机制,利用思维链(COT)促使确保自相矛盾的产出,从而防止在单一预测中同时出现情感相矛盾的词语. (3)我们设计了综观战略,一个大型语言模型(LLM)根据多个模型的相应评价分数来汇总预测,增强可靠性并减少情感冲突.
