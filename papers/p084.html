APPROACH · METHOD

方法思路

作者的核心方案是：我们把非盟>情绪推理作为逻辑、标签和结构上的非盟>情绪因子G之间的一个反事实-一致性问题，并提议FACR，以独立诱导的、极性-认知G为理由，并训练反事实-信仰目标：G标出某类因子的对非盟的干预必须移动预测，而一个标出不相关的因素则必须保持不变。问题—方法对应关系：显式组织表情、韵律、上下文或理由链，使结论能够对应到具体证据。

摘要证据： “We cast AU->emotion reasoning as a counterfactual-consistency problem between the rationale, the label, and a structural AU->emotion causal graph G, and propose FACR, which grounds the reasoner in an independently induced, polarity-aware G and trains a counterfactual-faithfulness objective: a do-intervention on an AU that G marks causal for a class must move the prediction, while one it marks irrelevant must leave it unchanged.”
